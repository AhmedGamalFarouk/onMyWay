# onMyWay · marketing site brief

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** The request was "a marketing site for an app like Uber, visually appealing, React."

- **Vibe:** a city at night, calm, sure of itself. Dark map, one electric lime accent.
- **Structure:** separate scenes, not one continuous world.
- **Assets:** none supplied, so everything is drawn in SVG and CSS. No generated images.
- **One action:** "Get the app".
- **What the visitor should believe:** you never have to wonder where your ride is.

## Feeling curve
1. Hero: confidence. Three planes of depth (map, phone, chip) move with the pointer and the scroll.
2. Tension: recognition. Old frustrations light up line by line, then "Not anymore."
3. **Trip (the peak):** control. Scrolling drives the car along a live route while the ETA counts down. This act gets the most scroll room (260%).
4. Safety: reassurance. A light panel wipes in after all the dark.
5. Rides: choice. Cards move sideways on desktop and swipe on phones.
6. Drive: freedom. A week picker that you can tap.
7. Close: resolution. "Where to?" repeats back whatever you type.

**Tell-someone sentence:** "It's the site where your scroll *is* the driver coming to pick you up."

No invented stats, prices or counters.
